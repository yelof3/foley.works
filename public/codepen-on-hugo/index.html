Adding Codepen Pens to Hugo Posts

A quick tutorial on how to display your Codepen pens on hugo posts

November 10, 2026

  • hugo
  • codepen
  • blog

Codepen on Hugo!⤴️

When I was building my Genuary post, I was poked around for a solution to embedding some Codepen pens on the post. For some reason I had no luck on my first search, but found a solution from this guy Alex on a follow-up search. All credit to Alex for the shortcode, but figured I’d share an overview of the solution here, largely because I wanted to learn codeblocks.

Steps for Placing Your Codepen on Hugo

  1. Download shortcode file

  2. Add file to your layouts/shortcodes folder

  3. Use this in your posts:

    {{<codepen id="{{your-codepen-pen-ID}}" author="{{your-codepen-ID}}">}}

    where {{your-codepen-pen-ID}} is the ID of your Codepen pen, and where {{your-codepen-ID}} is your actual Codepen account ID.

    You can also go with a more maximal version:

    {{<codepen id="{{your-codepen-pen-ID}}"
author="{{your-codepen-ID}}"
caption="Sample caption"
title="Sample Title"
height="400"
theme="light"
tab="html,result">}}

  4. Publish your post and see your pen in your post!
